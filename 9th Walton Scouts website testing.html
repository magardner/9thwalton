Welcome to the 9th Walton on Thames (Viscount) website, based in the Walton Methodist church,
our group has been providing fun and engaging activities for children aged 6-14 since 1958.

The group is run by a mix of leaders and young leaders, which give up their time
to create amazing oppotunities for the young children of the group.

The group does a minimum of 3 1-2 night camps per year, with occasional international trips in the U.K or abroad.
In 2023, 8 scouts, 2 young leaders and 2 leaders went to Kandersteg in Switzerland for the Kandersteg International Expedition.
